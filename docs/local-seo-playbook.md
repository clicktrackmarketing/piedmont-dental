## 3. Review generation (4.9 stars with 350+ Google reviews — a strength; keep velocity up, competitors in Oakland are adding reviews monthly)

**Direct review link (use in every request):**
`https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJRXRi3F2Hj4ARneUpH7lqi3I`
(Or generate the shorter `g.page/r/…` link in GBP → Ask for reviews.)

### Best practices
- **Ask at checkout** — the front desk asks while the visit is fresh, then the follow-up text goes out within the hour (via GHL on appointment completion).
- **Coach the wording** — invite patients to mention **the treatment and their city** ("teeth whitening in Piedmont", "Invisalign from Oakland"). Review text with service + geo terms directly helps those rankings.
- **Every patient, every visit** — consistency is the biggest lever on review volume.
- **Respond to 100%** of reviews, working service keywords into replies.
- **HIPAA rule for replies:** never confirm someone was a patient or mention any treatment detail they didn't write themselves. Thank them generically ("Thank you for the kind words about our whitening care") only when *they* named the treatment first; for negative reviews, reply once, offline the conversation, and never discuss care.
- **Text beats email** for response rate.

### SMS template (patients who opted in)
> Hi {first name}, thank you for visiting Piedmont Dental By Design today! If we earned it, a quick Google review means the world to Dr. Martenson, Dr. Ma, and the team: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJRXRi3F2Hj4ARneUpH7lqi3I — Reply STOP to opt out.
